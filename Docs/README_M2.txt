KungFu Crashout — Prototype README (Milestone 2)

Team 11 · Je-gnomes · GAM200 prototype



How to start
Phone: install the APK on an Android phone and open it, held landscape (tested on the Google Pixel 9a)
PC: open the Windows build folder and run KungFu Crashout.exe (full screen)
On the start screen tap Singleplayer
Character select: you play Monk Wu, the CPU plays Lord Yuan · tap READY
Level select: tap the Restaurant card · tap READY (the other stages are locked for now)
QUIT on the start screen (or Android Back / Esc) closes the game

Goal
Best of 3 rounds, 60 seconds each · win 2 rounds to win the match
Bring the opponent's health bar to zero, or have more health when the timer runs out
Key rule: martial arts on a standing opponent only turn their health red, and red health grows back after 3 seconds
To make damage stick: hit them with a thrown item (burns all their red health and knocks them down)
Then punish them on the floor with combos before they get up
The chair is the heavy item: slow throw, long knockdown · every other item is light: fast throw, short knockdown

Items
Stage items start asleep · attacks send out a shockwave that can wake them up
Blue glow = awake · yellow glow and thin outline = close enough to pick up
If no item is in play for a while, one drops in from above (a shadow shows where it lands)
Light items let you keep moving while held · the chair roots you in place

Controls on the phone (touch anywhere, no buttons)
Tap = light attack · keep tapping = 3-hit combo
Hold = heavy attack (knocks down)
Swipe left / right = dash (passes through the opponent)
Swipe up / up-left / up-right = jump
Tap while in the air = air combo (3 hits)
Two fingers held together = block (blocking drains stamina; when it runs out your guard breaks)
Two fingers just before a hit lands = parry (stuns the attacker; catches a thrown item)
Swipe + tap with another finger = special
  Sideways + tap = Elbow Slide (gap closer)
  Up + tap = Uppercut (launches the opponent; swipe up straight after to jump into the air combo)
  Down + tap = Leg Sweep (hits both sides)
Tap an item when close = pick it up · swipe from the held item = throw it
Dash out between enemy hits = combo breaker (escape a combo)
The top strip (health bars) ignores touches

Controls on PC (shown along the bottom of the fight)
A / D = dash · W = jump · Q / E = jump up-left / up-right
Left click = light attack · Right click = heavy attack
Space = block (tap it just before a hit to parry)
U / I / O = side / up / down special
F = pick up or drop an item · hold G + a direction, release G = throw
P or Esc = pause

Menus
Tap the diamond under the timer (or P / Esc on PC) to pause
Pause menu: Game tab (CPU difficulty Easy / Normal / Hard, CPU on or off), Audio tab (volume), Tuning tab (balance values for playtesting)
The gear icon on the start screen opens the same settings
The trophy on the start screen opens the Leaderboard: your wins, losses, win rate and best streak on this device
After a match: REMATCH, CHANGE FIGHTER or START (back to the start screen)
